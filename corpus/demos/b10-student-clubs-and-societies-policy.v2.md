# Student Clubs and Societies Policy

Synthetic test document written for CS4032 Assignment 03 (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES document.

## Registering a Society

A new student society needs at least twenty founding members from at least two batches, a faculty advisor, and a written constitution. The application is submitted to the Student Affairs Office in the first four weeks of the fall semester. A society is registered for one academic year and must renew its registration every fall.

## Elections

Each society elects a president, a general secretary and a treasurer every fall semester. Only students with a CGPA of 2.50 or above and no disciplinary record may stand for these posts. A student may hold an executive post in only one society at a time.

## Events and Funding

Events are approved by the Student Affairs Office at least two weeks in advance. Societies receive money from the Student Activities Fund in proportion to their approved annual plan. Every expense needs a receipt, and the treasurer submits the accounts at the end of each semester; a society that does not submit its accounts receives no funds in the next semester.

## Conduct

Society events end by 10 pm on campus. Outside guests need prior approval and must be registered at the gate. A society that breaks university rules can be suspended by the Student Affairs Office for up to one semester.
