## Who May Apply

A student who misses a sessional or final examination because of a documented medical emergency, the death of a close family member, or a clash with an approved university event may apply for a rescheduled examination. Travel plans, weddings and job interviews are not accepted as reasons.

## How to Apply

The application is submitted to the Academic Office within three working days of the missed examination, with the supporting documents: a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, a death certificate, or a letter from the event organiser. Late applications are not considered.

## Decision and Fee

The head of department decides the application within one week. If it is approved, the examination is held before the result is submitted, and the retake examination fee of the fee structure applies. If it is rejected, the missed examination is marked zero.
