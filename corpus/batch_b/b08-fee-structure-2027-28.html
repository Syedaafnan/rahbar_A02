Admission Fee

Admission Fee of Rs 35,000 is payable once at the time of admission. If a graduate of this University is admitted to a higher degree program, then admission fee is not payable again.

Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit of Rs 20,000 is also payable at the time of admission.

Tuition Fee (w.e.f. Fall 2027 semester)

Tuition Fee is payable in full before the start of each semester. For the academic year 2027-28 the tuition fee per credit hour applicable to all batches is as follows:

ProgramFee
BBA / BSRs. 13,500
MBA / MSRs. 13,500
PhDRs. 13,500

Student Activities Fund

Students contribute Rs 3,000 towards the Students Activities Fund per semester.
