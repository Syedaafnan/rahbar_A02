Applications for need-based financial assistance for the coming semester are open. Students who want to apply must fill in the financial assistance form, attach the salary certificate or income proof of their parents or guardian, the latest utility bills, and a copy of the last fee challan. Incomplete applications are not processed.

Shortlisted students are called for an interview with the financial aid committee. The committee may visit the applicant's home to verify the information. Assistance is given as a percentage of the tuition fee and is reviewed every semester; it is withdrawn if the student's CGPA falls below 2.50 or if any information in the application turns out to be false.

Merit scholarships are awarded automatically to the students with the highest SGPA in each batch and program, and no application is needed for them.
