# Library Borrowing Rules (revised wording)

Synthetic test document written for CS4032 Assignment 03 (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES document.

## Membership

Each enrolled student becomes a member of the campus library from the first day of the semester. The student ID card serves as the library card, and it has to be shown at the circulation desk for every loan. A lost card should be reported to the library immediately; books borrowed on a lost card stay the student's responsibility until the loss is reported.

## Borrowing Limits

Undergraduates may borrow up to four books at one time for a loan period of fourteen days. Graduate students may borrow up to six books for twenty-one days. Reference books, theses, bound journals and reserved course books may not leave the library. A loan may be renewed two times, online or at the desk, as long as no other member has put a hold on the book.

## Holds and Reserved Books

A member can place a hold on a book that is currently on loan. The book is kept at the desk for three working days after its return; after that the hold expires. Course books that an instructor puts on reserve may be used inside the library for two hours at a time.

## Overdue Fines

The fine for a late book is Rs. 50 per day for each book, including holidays. Borrowing privileges are suspended while unpaid fines are above Rs. 1,000. A lost or damaged book has to be replaced with a new copy, or paid for at its current price plus a processing charge of Rs. 500.

## Clearance

The library clearance certificate is needed before a degree, transcript or semester freeze is processed. A student who has a book on loan or an unpaid fine does not get clearance.
