# Library Borrowing Rules

Synthetic test document written for CS4032 Assignment 03 (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES document.

## Membership

Every enrolled student is a member of the campus library from the first day of the semester. The student ID card is the library card, and it must be shown at the circulation desk for every loan. A lost card must be reported to the library at once; books borrowed on a lost card remain the student's responsibility until the loss is reported.

## Borrowing Limits

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for a loan period of fourteen days. Graduate students may borrow up to six books for twenty-one days. Reference books, theses, bound journals and reserved course books cannot be taken out of the library. A loan can be renewed twice, online or at the desk, provided no other member has placed a hold on the book.

## Holds and Reserved Books

A member may place a hold on a book that is on loan. The book is kept at the desk for three working days after it is returned; after that the hold lapses. Course books that an instructor places on reserve can be used inside the library for two hours at a time.

## Overdue Fines

The fine for an overdue book is Rs. 50 per day per book, including holidays. Borrowing privileges are suspended while unpaid fines exceed Rs. 1,000. A lost or damaged book must be replaced with a new copy, or paid for at its current price plus a processing charge of Rs. 500.

## Clearance

The library clearance certificate is required before a degree, transcript or semester freeze is processed. A student with a book on loan or an unpaid fine does not receive clearance.
