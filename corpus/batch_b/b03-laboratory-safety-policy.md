# Laboratory Safety Policy

Synthetic test document written for CS4032 Assignment 03 (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES document.

## Scope

This policy applies to every student, teaching assistant and lab engineer working in the computing, electronics, physics and chemistry laboratories of the campus, including evening and weekend sessions.

## Protective Equipment

Safety goggles and closed shoes are compulsory in the electronics, physics and chemistry laboratories. Lab coats are compulsory in the chemistry laboratory. Long hair must be tied back when working near rotating machines or open flames. Food and drinks are not allowed in any laboratory, including the computing labs.

## Electrical Safety

Students must not open the casing of any computer, power supply or instrument. Faulty equipment is tagged "Do Not Use" and reported to the lab engineer. Circuits are powered only after the lab engineer or the instructor has checked the connections.

## Reporting Incidents

Every accident, injury, spill, fire or electrical fault must be reported to the lab engineer within one hour, even when nobody is hurt. The lab engineer records it in the incident register kept at the laboratory entrance and informs the head of department the same day. First-aid boxes and fire extinguishers are placed next to the door of every laboratory.

## Penalties

A student who breaks this policy can be asked to leave the laboratory for the rest of the session, and the lab work of that session is marked absent. Repeated violations are referred to the Disciplinary Committee.
